# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People and organizations planning weddings, corporate events, festivals, nightlife, and private events across Connecticut, the NYC metro, western Massachusetts, and the Northeast. Visitors need to quickly understand KNWLDG's fit, experience, service range, and availability before submitting a booking inquiry.

## Product Purpose

Present KNWLDG as a trusted, versatile open-format DJ and convert qualified visitors into booking inquiries. Success means visitors can recognize the event fit, understand the production support offered, and confidently start with their event date.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, polished, versatile. The experience should feel performance-led and confident while remaining clear enough for wedding, corporate, and private-event clients.

## Anti-references

Avoid soft, rounded wedding-template styling, generic nightlife neon, corporate SaaS card layouts, and visual choices that dilute the established square-edged, dark, magenta-to-violet identity.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the performer, atmosphere, and real event experience.
- Make trust and booking intent legible without flattening the brand personality.
- Use strong typography, disciplined grids, and square geometry as recognizable identity.
- Keep each section focused on one decision or proof point.
- Make date-first booking and event-fit information easy to find.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, semantic headings and form labels, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior for animated effects.
